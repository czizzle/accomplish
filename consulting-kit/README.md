# Bank AI Consulting Kit

A starter kit for offering community banks a one-time, flat-fee **AI Readiness Assessment** — a
"home inspection" style diagnostic delivered as a written report and read-out. Built for the
conversation with Collins State Bank (Wisconsin), but reusable for any community bank.

Open the `.html` files in a browser. Each is self-contained, works in light and dark mode, and
prints / exports to PDF cleanly.

## Files

| File | What it is | When you use it |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| `01-intake-and-engagement.html` | Engagement overview + the client intake form (sections A–G), the six scoring dimensions, and a plain-English breakdown of Fiserv vs. FDIC vs. OCC | Your working document; send the intake form once the NDA is signed |
| `02-proposal.html` | Client-facing proposal & pricing sheet — two tiers ($7,500 founding rate / $10,000), scope, timeline, signature block | Send to the bank president after the free scoping call |
| `03-engagement-letter-and-nda.html` | Fill-in-the-blank engagement letter + mutual NDA templates | The paperwork, signed before any bank data changes hands |

## Sequence

Scoping call → send **proposal** → client picks a tier → send **engagement letter + NDA** →
signed + 50% deposit → send **intake form** → discovery → report + read-out.

## Before you send anything

- **Fill the `[bracketed]` fields** — your name, firm/LLC, the client's details, dates.
- **Have a Wisconsin attorney review the legal templates** (letter + NDA). They are drafting aids,
  not legal advice — the limitation-of-liability and GLBA/confidentiality terms especially.
- **Stand up an entity + E&O insurance** before invoicing a bank; the engagement letter assumes both.

## Pricing rationale (summary)

Market benchmarks for AI readiness assessments in 2026: SMB $2k–$8k, focused mid-market $5k–$15k,
enterprise $15k–$50k+. A regulated community bank sits at the higher end of mid-market. Given a
first engagement with a warm relationship, **$7,500 founding-client rate** (standard $12k–$18k),
flat fee, 50% deposit — priced to be an easy yes and a credibility-builder, with implementation
work as a separate, later engagement.
