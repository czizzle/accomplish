The engagement in brief

Every vendor in the market is telling banks they "need an AI strategy." Almost none start with the honest, unglamorous first step: assessing what the bank already owns, what it's permitted to do with its data, and where staff time is actually being lost. This engagement is that inspection — the equivalent of a home inspection before you commit to a renovation. You receive a written findings report and a prioritized roadmap. Not a sales pitch, and no obligation to buy anything.

02

What you receive

  • Current-state findings report — a plain-English read on strengths, gaps, and the real (often unofficial) AI use already happening inside the bank.
  • Readiness scorecard — the bank rated across six dimensions: data & infrastructure, technology fit, security & compliance, people & change readiness, governance, and opportunity value.
  • Prioritized opportunity roadmap — quick wins separated cleanly from strategic bets, with anything touching credit decisions or customer data flagged for the guardrails it needs.
  • Vendor-landscape snapshot — build vs. buy vs. use-what-you-already-pay-for.
  • Live read-out — a working session to walk leadership (and the board, if desired) through the findings.

What this engagement is not

This is a diagnostic, not an implementation. It does not include building, configuring, or deploying any system, and it is not legal, compliance, or accounting advice — the bank remains responsible for its own regulatory decisions. If the findings point to work worth doing, a separate implementation proposal will be provided, priced on its own terms.
